Live $0.016/min selected

The system listens and writes the transcript down while the therapist and client are still talking. It's complete the instant the session ends.

  • Use it whensomething needs to happen during the session — live safety-word flagging, live captions, or any tool that has to react to what's said in the moment.
  • Why it costs moreGoogle has to hold a continuous connection open and process audio as it arrives, which is priced as a premium "streaming" service.
  • Data footprintno separate recording has to be made — audio is processed and discarded as it streams, so nothing extra is stored beforehand.
Batch ≈$0.004/min* selected

The session is recorded first. Once it's over, that recording is sent off and transcribed afterward — the write-up isn't ready right away, it comes back later.

  • Use it whenthe transcript is only needed afterward — session notes, documentation, billing records — nobody needs the words the instant they're spoken.
  • Why it costs lessroughly a quarter of the live price, because Google processes it as a stored file on its own schedule rather than a live feed.
  • The trade-offthere's a delay before the transcript exists (minutes to longer, depending on length and processing load), and it can't support anything that needs to happen live.
  • Data footprintthis mode needs a recording of the session to exist, even briefly. Recording a session is a separate thing to get patient consent for, on top of transcribing it, and needs its own privacy sign-off — the lower price comes with more paperwork, not less.

In short: live costs more because you're paying for the transcript to exist the instant it's spoken. Batch costs less, but the trade is a delay before it's ready, plus an extra consent/privacy step for keeping a recording in the first place. * batch rate is an estimate — see notes below.

Notes on these figures

  • Rates sourced from Google's official pricing pages, verified 7 Jul 2026. Both Chirp and the Gemini 2.5 series price identically regardless of region — europe-west4 (NL) costs the same as global, so residency is free here.
  • Transcription volume tiers ($0.016 → $0.010 → $0.008 → $0.004 per minute) are applied as graduated bands past 500k / 1M / 2M minutes a month, the standard model for usage-based cloud pricing — worth confirming against Google's actual billing behaviour if volume gets close to a threshold.
  • The batch/async rate ($0.004/min) is carried over from the top volume tier as a placeholder — confirm against Google's actual asynchronous Speech-to-Text SKU before relying on it. (See the Live vs. Batch comparison above for what else differs between the two.)
  • Figures are model-inference cost only: orchestration (e.g. n8n), storage, logging and human-review time are not included.
  • The € figure is a simple conversion at the rate entered above — edit it to the live rate for precision.
