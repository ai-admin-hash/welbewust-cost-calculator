Everything on Google Cloud — and what that really does for NEN 7510

Moving the whole platform to Google Cloud in the Netherlands fixes our two worst data problems today and gives us a clean foundation to certify on. It does not, by itself, hand us the NEN 7510 certificate. Here is the honest split — what the move settles, and what stays our job.

Why the move alone isn't the finish line

Two tracks, and only one of them is Google's

NEN 7510 is a standard for how a healthcare organisation runs its security — not a badge a cloud can carry for us. So the work splits in two.

Handled by the move

The technical layer ~20%

  • Patient data kept in the EU / Netherlands
  • Encryption at rest and in transit
  • A certified cloud supplier ISO 27001, healthcare hosting
  • A single, auditable path for every AI decision
  • The technical ability to log who opened what

This is what "everything on Google Cloud" buys us. Real — but it's the floor.

Still ours to do

The organisational layer ~80%

  • The information-security management system — how we run security day to day
  • A formal risk assessment / DPIA on the patient data
  • Access logging to the NEN 7513 standard our app's job — Google supplies none
  • Staff policy, training, incident response, continuity
  • Signed processor agreements with every supplier
  • The independent certification audit

None of this comes from a cloud. It needs a NEN consultant — which we have not yet lined up.

The single biggest win

All AI → Gemini. One catch.

Switching every AI to Gemini is the most valuable compliance move on the list — it ends patient text and audio being sent to the United States. But "Gemini" only counts if we use Google's enterprise EU path (Vertex AI, a European region).

The free consumer Gemini app or API key does not keep data in Europe and cannot touch patient data. Same brand, different contract — and that difference is the whole point.

The part that's easy to miss

Don't forget the back office

Our automations quietly handle patient data across screening, intake, letters to GPs, and email — and today they lean on Google Sheets as a filing cabinet and still call US AI. Moving them onto Google Cloud is necessary but not sufficient: the Sheets-as-database habit and the US AI calls have to go too, or the app is compliant while the workflows behind it are not.

Google Sheets is only acceptable inside a proper Workspace account with a signed agreement — never a personal Google login.

Who is responsible for what

What Google gives us — and what stays ours

Google gives us

  • ✓ Patient data kept in the EU / Netherlands
  • ✓ Encryption, key management, physical security
  • ✓ A signed processor agreement covers AVG/GDPR art. 28
  • ✓ Certificates to show an auditor ISO 27001, 27017/27018, HDS health-hosting
Caveat we must not get wrong: Google does not hold a NEN 7510 certificate. We lean on its ISO / health-hosting certificates instead. We must never tell an auditor "our cloud is NEN 7510 certified" — it isn't.

Stays ours

  • ○ The management system + the audit that earns the certificate
  • ○ The DPIA / risk assessment
  • ○ NEN 7513 access logging, inside our own app
  • ○ Contracts with every other supplier Zivver, …
  • ○ Staff training, policy, incident drills
  • ○ A NEN consultant for certification we have an FG, Maaike — not yet a consultant

The decisions and the real cost

Running it ~€250–400/mo infrastructure to host everything. AI usage scales with volume — see the calculator.
Getting certified NEN consultant + audit the new line item this plan introduces. This — not the cloud — is the real first invoice.

Four build decisions — resolved:

  1. Database — start on a single-datacenter database; upgrade to a mirrored, guaranteed-uptime one before large patient volumes. Data is safe either way. ✓ Decided: lean now, revisit.
  2. Office AI box — retired. All AI moves to Gemini Flash. ✓ Decided: ~€700/mo → ~€2/mo.
  3. AI location — NL-pinned everywhere possible; the AI model runs EU-wide — the one thing Google can't keep NL-only. ✓ Decided: EU is the legal bar.
  4. EU guardrail — switch on the free, enforced EU-only boundary now; add the paid EU-support-staff tier later only if the auditor asks. ✓ Decided: free tier now.

Bottom line

Yes — we can run the whole platform, the automations, and all the AI on Google Cloud in the Netherlands, and we should: it removes our two worst data risks today and builds a clean foundation. But "everything on Google Cloud" is the foundation for NEN 7510, not the certificate. The certificate is organisational work plus an independent audit — and the first real cost there is a consultant, not the cloud.
