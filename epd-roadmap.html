The one rule

Build the billing core last — and integrate it, don't build it. Claims, VECOZO and yearly ZPM validation are the hardest, riskiest 20% (a 2025 rule change nearly broke claim payments practice-wide). Outsource that 20% (GZ-entry is free + VECOZO-certified); build only what's actually your product — the dossier, the e-health, the workflow, the panel.

What this really is

A product bet, not this year's cost-cut.

Finishing the diagram turns Aweris from a €30/seat e-health add-on into a €43–67/seat full EPD — a CareWeb competitor. That's the prize. But it's 12–18 months of regulated build, and you own compliance forever. Fund it from e-health revenue.

The sequence

Four phases — sellable and solvent at every step

Phase 0 — Have it

Now · the sellable product

The e-health layer that's already live and can be licensed to other practices today.

Patient portal · ROM & screening · 304 modules · messages & tasks · documents & audit · Zivver · n8n + AI.

Phase 1 — Low-risk wins

≈ 4–7 dev-weeks · near-zero regulatory risk

Real value with no records/billing exposure — safe to ship while you sell Phase 0.

WeFact finance automations (no-show, dunning) · Video (self-hosted Jitsi) · unified Admin workspace over the existing n8n flows.

Phase 2 — The record decision

≈ 4–6 months · the point of no return

Where Aweris becomes an EHR, not a companion. This is the point of no return.

Client dossier with NAW/BSN (+ SBV-Z) · Agenda & locking · ZorgDomein referrals.

Reverses the deliberate no-BSN design and takes on WGBO 20-year retention + full NEN 7513 authorization tiers. Do this only once the product bet is decided.

Phase 3 — The billing core

≈ 4–6 mo — or ~6–8 wks via GZ-entry · build last

The declaratie engine — highest risk, permanent compliance ownership.

ZPM & validation · Claims (integrate GZ-entry) · VECOZO + Vektis.

Owns the yearly ZPM rule churn; a missed release means practices don't get paid. Only worth crossing once revenue funds it — and even then, integrate the claims engine rather than build it.

The build-vs-buy rule

Don't build the declaratie engine. Integrate GZ-entry (free, VECOZO-certified) for claims, WeFact for invoicing, Jitsi for video, Zivver for secure mail. Build only what is genuinely your product: the dossier, the e-health, the workflow, the clinician panel.

The 20% that is hardest — VECOZO claims + the yearly ZPM compliance chase — is exactly the 20% to let someone else own. It's a cost centre with legal risk, not a differentiator.

Bottom line

The picture is right — and reachable — but it's a 12–18-month product bet to become a CareWeb competitor, not a cost-cut. So: sell the Phase-0 e-health layer now, ship the Phase-1 low-risk wins, and only cross into Phases 2–3 when revenue and a real product decision justify committing to insurer billing and 20-year records. The near-term €6.244 doesn't wait for any of this — it comes from selling what's already live.
