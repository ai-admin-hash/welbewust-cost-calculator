What this is

This is a planning workspace, not the product. The Aweris platform itself lives at aweris.nl; these pages are where we work out the decisions behind moving WelBewust onto it.

Every page is a single, self-contained file — no build step, no accounts, no tracking. Open one in a browser and it just runs. Numbers are editable in place so anyone can pressure-test an assumption without asking a developer.

Treat everything here as a working document: a shared, honest draft — not legal or financial advice.
